The public Java client currently documents NFSv3 support. The Commercial Edition offers NFSv4 and Kerberos under a separate commercial license.

Which license should I choose?

Can I redistribute it with my application?

Commercial redistribution is permitted under the applicable license terms, with no runtime royalties. Contact us to review the terms for your distribution model.

Are features different between plans?

No. Both commercial licenses include NFSv4 and Kerberos functionality. The distinction is personal and independent commercial development versus organizational use.

What should I confirm before purchasing?

Send us your Java runtime, NFS server, Kerberos setup, and intended use. Ask about compatibility, delivery, renewal, and any support requirements so you can review the details before committing.
